
**Last updated: 28 September 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Rank Sarthi Next Gen Private Limited**
("Rank Sarthi", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, stores and protects
personal information when you use **ranksarthi.com**, create an account,
use our academic resources, assessments, calculators, test-preparation
features, contact forms or other Rank Sarthi services.

Our services are primarily designed for students preparing for examinations
such as JEE, NEET and NDA, as well as parents, educators and institutes.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account information

When you create or use a Rank Sarthi account, we may collect information such
as your:

- name
- email address
- mobile number
- login credentials
- class or academic level
- target examination
- city
- school or institute
- parent or guardian information where applicable

Passwords, where used, should be stored using secure one-way hashing and not
as readable plain-text passwords.

### Academic and assessment information

When you use tests, practice tools, diagnostics, study planners or other
academic features, we may collect information such as:

- questions attempted
- answers submitted
- correct and incorrect responses
- unanswered questions
- scores
- time spent
- test completion information
- subject and topic performance
- self-assessment information
- study goals
- progress information
- performance analytics generated from your activity

Where Rank Sarthi provides automated or AI-assisted academic analysis, the
system may process relevant academic-performance information in order to
generate learning insights or recommendations.

We aim to limit the personal information supplied to external processing
services where reasonably possible.

### Institute-provided information

Where a school, coaching institute or other educational organisation provides
access to Rank Sarthi for its students, the institute may provide information
such as:

- student name
- contact information
- class
- batch or cohort
- student identifier
- parent or guardian contact information where appropriate

The organisation providing such information is responsible for ensuring that
it is authorised to provide the information to Rank Sarthi.

Where applicable, authorised institutes may receive academic-performance and
progress information relating to students enrolled through that institute.

### Contact and enquiry information

When you contact Rank Sarthi through our contact form, email or telephone, we
may collect:

- name
- email address
- mobile number
- enquiry category
- message
- organisation or institute details, where applicable
- correspondence required to respond to your enquiry

### Payment and transaction information

If you purchase a Rank Sarthi subscription, test series, package, top-up or
other paid service, payment processing may be handled by an authorised
third-party payment provider.

Rank Sarthi may retain transaction information such as:

- order reference
- payment status
- amount
- purchased product or plan
- invoice details
- refund status

Rank Sarthi does not intend to store full card numbers, UPI credentials,
banking passwords or similar payment authentication credentials where these
are processed directly by the payment provider.

### Technical and usage information

When you use the website or platform, we may automatically receive technical
information such as:

- IP address
- browser and device type
- operating system
- pages visited
- referring page or source
- date and time of access
- application or server errors
- session and security information
- performance and diagnostic logs

Where analytics tools are enabled, they may be used to understand how the
website and platform are used and to improve performance and usability.

## 2. How We Use Information

We may use information collected through Rank Sarthi to:

- create and manage user accounts
- authenticate users and maintain account security
- provide academic content, assessments and platform functionality
- calculate test scores and performance statistics
- provide study-planning or academic-performance information
- generate automated or AI-assisted learning insights where such functionality
  is available
- personalise academic recommendations and resources
- provide institutional reporting where a student is legitimately enrolled
  through an institute
- process purchases, payments and refunds
- send necessary transactional communications
- respond to enquiries and support requests
- investigate suspected misuse, fraud or unauthorised access
- protect Rank Sarthi's content and platform integrity
- diagnose technical problems
- improve the website, platform and academic experience
- comply with applicable legal, accounting and regulatory obligations

We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

## 3. How Information May Be Shared

We may share information only where reasonably necessary for operating Rank
Sarthi, including with:

### Service providers

Technology, infrastructure, hosting, communications, analytics, payment or
other service providers that process information on our behalf.

### Educational institutes

Where a student receives access through an institute, authorised institute
personnel may receive relevant student progress or assessment information.

They do not receive the student's password or payment authentication
credentials.

### Legal and regulatory requirements

We may disclose information where required by applicable law, a court,
regulator or other legally authorised authority.

### Business protection

Information may be used or disclosed where reasonably necessary to protect
Rank Sarthi, its users, intellectual property, platform security or legal
rights.

## 4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Rank Sarthi may use cookies or similar technologies to:

- keep users signed in
- maintain session and security functionality
- remember preferences
- understand website usage
- improve website and platform performance

Where legally required, consent mechanisms may be used for non-essential
cookies or analytics technologies.

Browser settings may also allow you to control or delete certain cookies.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for
the purpose for which it was collected, including:

- maintaining active accounts
- providing purchased services
- maintaining academic progress
- resolving disputes
- processing refunds
- maintaining security records
- complying with financial, legal and regulatory obligations

Different categories of information may have different retention periods.

Where an account is deleted or deactivated, some information may need to be
retained for legitimate legal, financial, security or compliance purposes
before being securely deleted or anonymised.

## 6. Children and Users Under 18

Many Rank Sarthi users may be under 18 years of age.

If you are under 18, you should use Rank Sarthi with the knowledge and
permission of your parent or legal guardian.

Parents and guardians should review the information supplied by a student and
the student's use of the platform where appropriate.

Where applicable law requires parental or guardian consent for the processing
of a child's personal information, Rank Sarthi may require appropriate
authorisation before providing relevant services.

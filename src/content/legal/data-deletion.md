Rank Sarthi respects your privacy. If you have used Rank Sarthi or connected a
Facebook or Instagram account through our services, you may request deletion
of personal information associated with your use of Rank Sarthi.

## 1. How to Request Data Deletion

**Step 1:** Email info@ranksarthi.com with the subject "User Data Deletion
Request – Rank Sarthi".

**Step 2:** Include your registered email address, your Rank Sarthi account
name (if applicable), and whether your request relates to your Rank Sarthi
account, Facebook, Instagram or another connected Meta service. Specify
whether you want particular personal data deleted or your account deleted.

**Step 3:** Our team will review your request and may ask for reasonable
verification before proceeding. Never send passwords, OTPs or access tokens.

**Step 4:** Once verified, we will process your request in accordance with
applicable law and our Privacy Policy, and notify you when it has been
completed or if additional information is required.

For a minor's account, a parent or legal guardian may submit a request,
subject to appropriate verification.

## 2. What Information Can Be Deleted?

Subject to the information actually held by Rank Sarthi and applicable
retention obligations, a verified request may cover:

- Account profile and contact details
- Facebook or Instagram account identifiers or connection information held by Rank Sarthi, if applicable
- Associated access tokens, if stored
- Academic activity and assessment information linked to the account
- Other personal data associated with the account

Certain records may need to be retained where required by applicable law,
financial obligations, security, fraud prevention or dispute resolution. Such
exceptions will be explained where applicable.

## 3. Remove Rank Sarthi from Facebook

If you connected Rank Sarthi through Facebook:

- Open Facebook Settings & Privacy → Settings.
- Go to Apps and Websites.
- Find Rank Sarthi, if listed, and select Remove.
- To request deletion of personal information separately held by Rank Sarthi, follow the email instructions above.

Removing an app from Facebook does not necessarily erase information
independently stored by Rank Sarthi. Deletion requests should also be sent
to us.

## 4. Processing Time

We review requests promptly after receipt. We will provide a completion update
or explain any additional verification or lawful retention requirement.
